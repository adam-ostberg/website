## How it started

I have always had an interest in technology, but it really bloomed during a study year in Oxford. I was there for one year in ninth grade and received the schools Computer Award. Since then I have been drawn to both game and web development. Unfortunately most of my early game development work was from before I learned about git and GitHub, so it is lost to time.

I chose a technical track at gymnasium (upper secondary school) and finished it by building a fighting robot with a friend for a competition between schools across Sweden. We took second place.

## Media Technology

In 2023 I started a bachelor's in Media Technology at KTH. It suited me because it mixed programming and math with design and the creative side of things. It also focused somewhat on machine learning, which later became a larger interest of mine.

## Finding data and machine learning

Over the course of the degree I kept drifting towards data. In a machine learning course, a few classmates and I [trained models to predict](/projects/student-dropout-prediction/) whether a student graduates or drops out, mostly because we wanted to know what a model would say about us. For my bachelors thesis we built [Matjakt](/projects/matjakt/), which scrapes millions of grocery prices and works out the cheapest way to buy a whole shopping list.

## Now

In 2026 I started a master's in computer science on the Data Science track, with a machine learning specialization. Alongside it I'm interning at an AI startup, annotating the video data their models learn from.

## Outside of computer science

I love to travel. In the winter I go skiing as much as I can, and in the summer I'm probably somewhere in the mountains too, either hiking or mountain biking. I also really like playing poker.
